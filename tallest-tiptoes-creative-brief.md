# Tallest Tiptoes — Creative Brief

---

## Brand Identity

**Name:** Tallest Tiptoes
**Signature:** Script/handwritten logo animation that writes itself across the screen
**Ballet reference:** Subtle, in the name — not literal ballet imagery

---

## Color Palette (Final Direction)

**Foundation:**
- Cream / warm off-white (like old paper, linen, parchment)
- Black (ink, strong, dominant for text and structure)

**Accent:**
- Terracotta / brick red — warm, earthy, not wine-dark (reference: Atut Moni torn paper, the contacts page with sketched flowers, Paul Klee reds, Louise Bourgeois red hands)
- Old Rose #B98589 — supporting warmth, not dominant

**Avoid:**
- Green-heavy anything
- Too much purple
- Anything too muted or washed out
- Anything too girly or pastel

**Mood:** Antique warmth, artist's desk, Parisian flea market, hand-drawn meets editorial, collected not curated

---

## Typography

**Display / Headings:** DM Serif Display (or similar elegant serif)
**Body / UI:** Cormorant Garamond
**Signature / Logo:** Dancing Script (or custom hand-lettered)

Font inspiration: Eleven Eleven Sporting Club script, heritage sporting club feel but feminine

---

## Visual Language

**Key Design Elements:**
- Hand-drawn line work (Calder-like swirls, Paul Klee sketches, pencil flourishes)
- Flower sketches in ink or red as decorative elements
- Torn paper edges, collage textures
- Old paper / parchment feel
- Small scattered images on cream backgrounds (Chloe Benjamin website style)
- Monospace type for labels/tags mixed with serif for headings
- Black ink on cream as the primary text treatment
- Red/terracotta as accent, not overwhelming

**Reference Sites:**
- Chloe Benjamin (scattered images on cream, serif type, floral icon)
- Atut Moni (torn paper, mix of hand-drawn and photography, terracotta red)
- The contacts page with red flower sketches on cream
- D.Vine Shop (antique objects, brass, old paper, still life photography)
- Sensitive and Naive Gaze editorial (white background, scattered photos, hand-drawn script overlapping images, small circular cutouts)

**Art References:**
- Paul Klee (red washes, whimsical line drawings, stars)
- Louise Bourgeois (red hands on manuscript paper)
- Hilma af Klint (abstract line work)
- Calder (wire-like continuous line drawings)
- Meghann Stephenson (still life paintings — coupe glasses, drooping tulips on black)
- Olive brushstroke illustration on blush wash

**Photography Style:**
- Still life, collected objects, brass, old things
- Mirror selfie outfits (Getting Dressed)
- Travel finds, flea market treasures
- Warm, natural light, not overly styled

## Site Architecture

### Navigation
The Forum · Getting Dressed · Collected · His Not Hers · My Guides

### 01 — The Forum
Editorial hub. One piece of content drops on a set schedule.
- Content types: written essays, video/vlogs, movie recs, podcast, photo essays
- Filter by format
- Comments on every post
- Schedule: Thursday or Sunday at 8 PM (cozy in bed content) + Monday 8 AM for lighter drops

### 02 — Getting Dressed
Quick outfit posts, mirror selfie grid.
- Pinterest-style masonry layout with offset columns
- Filter by mood: errands, dinner, weekend, airport, night out, work
- Tap to expand into full outfit breakdown with each piece listed
- Shoppable where possible
- Can cross-promote Collected items naturally

### 03 — Collected (name TBD, still marinating)
Personal shop — not third-party resale.
- Clean e-commerce grid (Roweam/Hikone inspired), NOT masonry
- Three sub-categories:
  - Made by Me — evening bags, sewn pieces (1 of 1)
  - Collected — vacation finds (barrettes, belts, ceramics)
  - From My Closet — occasional personal pieces
- Story-driven: where items came from matters
- Process photos for Made by Me items
- Sold Out / Coming Soon / Notify Me states
- Drop notification email signup
- Drop-based releases, not always-on

### 04 — His Not Hers
Men's section — clean, direct, no articles.
- Three categories:
  - Splurge — investment pieces ("Go big")
  - Wardrobe Basics — essentials ("Start here")
  - Outfit Guide — full looks laid out ("Full looks, no guessing")
- Product grid with one-line notes
- Outfit Guide shows 4 pieces per look with "Shop This Look"
- Occasions: Date Night, Weekend, Wedding Guest, Office
- Tagline: "We did the research. You just pick."
- Quick Picks horizontal scroll on landing page
- Email signup: "No spam, just picks"

### 05 — My Guides
Recommendations and lifestyle.
- Four sub-categories: Restaurants, Hosting, Weekend, Travel
- Category grid as filter on landing page
- Restaurant guides: by neighborhood, one-line takes, photos
- Hosting guides: sections for The Table, The Menu, The Playlist, The Timeline
- Travel guides: day-by-day with morning/afternoon/evening
- Weekend: event/activity list with why it's worth it
- Comments on every guide

### Special Features

**The Honesty Box**
- Anonymous submission form
- Questions, confessions, hot takes
- Curate and publish monthly as a Forum post
- Black card with wine/red accent, "SUBMIT" CTA

**Subscription Box (Future / Back Burner)**
- Monthly mystery box
- 3 hints dropped throughout the month
- Could include makeup, pasta kit with recipe, accessories
- Subscribers opt in or skip each month based on hints
- Name ideas: "The Parcel," "The Monthly," "The Find"

---

## Content Schedule

**Monday 8-9 AM:** Lighter content — Getting Dressed post, weekend recap vlog, quick Guide rec
**Thursday or Sunday 8 PM:** Heavier content — Forum essay, longer video, deep dive
**Drop Days (TBD):** Shop releases, His Not Hers spotlights, subscription box hints

**Principle:** Serialize content. Same day, same time, every drop. Consistency beats frequency. Audience should know when to check in like waiting for a favorite show.
